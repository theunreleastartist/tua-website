Who This Is For

Two kinds of artists walk through this door.

01
You're working through something.

Self-doubt. Perfectionism. Fear of being seen. Creative blocks that keep showing up no matter how much you know or how hard you work. You've got the talent. You need someone in your corner who actually gets the music life, not just the self-help version of it.

02
You're building toward something.

You're not blocked. You're moving. But you want a thinking and action partner who can help you go further, faster, and with more clarity than you'd get alone. Strategy, accountability, honest perspective. Someone invested in where you're going, not just where you've been.

How It Works

Built around your life. Not a template.

Every creative's situation is different. The format, pace, and focus of our work together gets shaped around what you actually need, not a fixed program. Here's how we can engage.

1:1 Coaching

Regular sessions dedicated to your goals, your work, and your growth. Deep, consistent, and fully personalized to where you are and where you're going.

Intensive Sessions

Focused deep-dives for creatives who need momentum on something specific. One or a few sessions built around a clear objective and real outcomes.

Inner Circle

A lower-cost membership for those who want to stay connected to this work without 1:1 coaching right now. Community, resources, and direct access, starting at $19 a month.

Spots for 1:1 work are intentionally limited. If you're curious, the discovery call is the place to start.

About

This isn't a side project for me.

Singer / Songwriter Producer Multi-Instrumentalist Professional Coach Educator Program Director Technologist

I'm a professional and executive coach, a singer, songwriter, producer, and multi-instrumentalist. I've spent years working as a program director, college educator, and mentor in the music and creative industries. I've lived inside this world from every angle.

I built this because I kept seeing talented artists held back by things that had nothing to do with talent. The self-doubt. The fear. The perfectionism. The not-quite-knowing-why-you're-stuck. Those things are real, and they're fixable. That's what this work is for.

I treat this the way I treat music. With real investment. With care about whether it actually works. Not like a job.
