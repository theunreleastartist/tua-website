Work With Me

There are different ways to work together.

The shape of the work is built around you — your life, your goals, your creative process, and what you actually need right now.

Whether you are working through something that has kept you stuck, building toward something meaningful, or navigating a creative decision with no one who truly understands what you are carrying, there is a way in.

Why this work exists

You do not have to carry this alone.

Many music creatives spend years feeling misunderstood. Not because the people around them do not care, but because creative work is deeply personal.

Music creatives often experience the world differently. They see possibilities, connections, ideas, and futures that may not make sense to everyone else yet. The songs, projects, and dreams they carry often exist long before anyone else can see them.

Thinking partnership is built for that reality: a place where both the creative and the work are understood.

The inner work and the actual work. We do both. I work with you on what is in the way, on what needs strengthening, and on what you are building. We go deep on all of it and I stay with you until it is finished.

Whether that means seeing one thing through to the end or simply having someone genuinely alongside you for as long as you need — both are exactly why this exists.

Because sometimes the most powerful thing is not advice. It is being genuinely understood by someone who knows what it is like to carry meaningful creative work through real life.

How the work happens

Not a formula. A relationship built around the person and the work.

The work is never predetermined. It comes out of an honest first conversation about where you are, what you are building or working through, and what kind of support would actually serve you.

One
A focused session on something specific.

Sometimes you have one decision, block, question, or creative issue that needs space and perspective. We go deep, identify what is really happening, and you leave with clarity and a real next step.

Two
Ongoing support alongside the work over time.

This is for the music creative who needs more than one session. We build real plans, track progress, work through resistance, and stay connected between sessions so the work keeps moving.

Three
Embedded in a specific project from start to done.

If you are building, launching, releasing, or finishing something specific, I am alongside it from the first conversation through completion. Inner work and outer execution run together all the way to done.

What support looks like

High-touch does not mean more pressure. It means more genuine support.

The work is designed around your pace, your process, and the support that helps you move forward without losing connection to why the work matters.

Client Portal
A place to track the journey.

Clients can add pre-session notes, message between sessions, track goals and progress, and update the plan as the work evolves. Think of it like a project plan for your creative journey.

Between Sessions
You are not left on your own.

Support can include check-ins, questions, notes, reflection, encouragement, or processing what is coming up as you move through the work in real time.

The Work Itself
I get to know what you are making.

Songs, projects, mixes, videos, ideas, goals, and the vision behind them. I take the time to understand both the creative and the work so the support is rooted in your actual reality.

Actionable Progress
Every session moves something forward.

Sessions include inner work, reflection, planning, and clear next steps. The goal is not just insight. The goal is helping you move meaningful work forward in a way that can last.

A small number of clients

The level of attention is intentional.

I work with a small number of people at any one time. That is intentional. The level of attention, investment, and genuine presence I bring to each person requires it.

The price and structure follow from the conversation, because the support should reflect what would actually help you. If you are considering whether this is right for you, the first conversation is the place to find out.

This is not a framework applied to whoever sits in front of me. It is built around you. Your work. Your vision. Your way of moving through things.
