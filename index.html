What I eventually understood is that the reason things don't move is almost never the reason that looks like the reason. It goes beneath the surface. To the beliefs, the fears, the patterns, the identity. That's where the real work is.

But the inner work alone isn't enough. The structure. The accountability. Someone who understands the work, the roles, and the real effort they require. That's what The Unreleased Artist delivers. The inner work and the outer plan. Both. Not one without the other.
