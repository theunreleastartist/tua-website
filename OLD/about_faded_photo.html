About Me

Why This Work Matters To Me

I built The Unreleased Artist from my own experience.

For years, I balanced a professional career, family, and a deep commitment to creating music. Like many creatives, I often felt like I was carrying the work alone.

I know what it's like to have a song in your heart and a million things on your plate. To care deeply about your work and question whether you have enough time, clarity, confidence, or support to keep going.

I also know that you do not have to choose between a meaningful life and making art.

There is a way to build a sustainable creative life that supports who you are and what matters most.

That's why I do this work: to walk alongside music creatives so they can stop carrying it all alone and create with more clarity, confidence, and momentum.

“More than anything, I’m proud that I never stopped creating.”

What I Believe

You do not need more hustle. You need a better relationship with the work and a more sustainable way to move it forward.

Your art deserves the same care and commitment you give to everything else in your life.

The world needs your voice, your story, and your creativity. Let's make sure it gets out.

Creativity is not selfish.

It is part of how you process, contribute, connect, and serve.

Clarity creates momentum.

When you understand what is really getting in the way, the next step becomes easier to see.

You do not have to do it alone.

You just need the right kind of support for the person you are and the work you are carrying.
