Work With Me

There are different ways to work together.

Each one is built entirely around you. Your goals, your life, your definition of success, and what you actually need right now.

Whether you are working through something that has kept you stuck, building toward something and wanting a genuine thinking and action partner alongside you, or navigating a decision with no one who truly understands what you are carrying — there is a way in.

The shape of the work is not predetermined. It comes out of a real conversation about where you are and what you actually need. Not a menu you pick from. A relationship built around you.

Working through something
Something is in the way and you cannot quite name what it is.

We go beneath the surface of what is actually getting in the way. Not the version of it that looks like the reason. The real one underneath. And then we build the actual plan to move through it.

Building toward something
You are moving. You just want someone genuinely alongside it.

A thinking and action partner invested in where you are going. Someone who helps you navigate decisions, holds you accountable to what matters, and stays genuinely present for the whole journey — not just the difficult moments.

What the work can look like

The work takes different shapes depending on what you need.

None of these are packages from a brochure. They are descriptions of what working together actually looks like at different levels of depth and commitment. Which one is right comes out of the first conversation.

One
One focused session on something specific.

Sometimes you have one thing you need to work through or think through with someone who actually understands the context. A decision. A block. A question you have been sitting with and cannot get clear on alone.

One session. We go deep. You leave with clarity and a real next step. No commitment required beyond that.

Two
Ongoing support alongside the work over time.

For the music creative who needs more than one session. Whether you are working through something that runs deeper or building something that needs consistent presence alongside it — this is the ongoing partnership.

Real plans. Real progress tracking. Real accountability from someone who understands the work, the roles, and the real effort they require. Thinking things through together. Staying invested between sessions not just during them.

This is what a genuine thinking and action partner looks like over time. Built around your pace, your process, and your way of moving through things.

Three
Embedded in a specific project from start to done.

You have something specific to build, launch, or finish. I am alongside it from the first conversation to the moment it is out. Not just coaching the difficult moments when they appear. Present for the whole thing.

I know your project, what it means to you, and what is in the way. We build the plan together, track every step, and work through every block as it comes up. Inner work and outer execution running simultaneously. All the way to done.

"This is not a framework applied to whoever sits in front of me. It is built entirely around you. Your work. Your vision. Your way of moving through things."

The shape of how we work together is never predetermined. It comes out of an honest first conversation about where you are, what you are building or working through, and what kind of support would actually serve you.

Not here are your three options. Here is what I think would actually help you and why. That recommendation is specific to you. Not to a category you fit into.

The price and the structure follow from that conversation. Not from a list on a website.

I work with a small number of people at any one time. That is intentional. The level of attention, investment, and genuine presence I bring to each person requires it. If you are considering whether this is right for you — the first conversation is the place to find out.

No pitch. No pressure. Just an honest conversation about where you are, what you are working through or building toward, and whether working together makes sense. That conversation tells both of us what the right shape of the work is.
