What this is and what it isn't
The Inner Circle gives you the insight. The work of applying it is still yours to do.

This membership is built around one thing — my consistent presence in your creative life. My voice, my perspective, my understanding of what it costs to do this alone. Every week, every month, reliably there.

It is not a coaching relationship. It is not a course. It does not replace the structured tools, workbooks, or resources I create as standalone products. Think of it as the closest thing to having someone who truly understands your world in your corner — before you're ready for the full 1:1 experience.

Insight is what you get here. The tools for working through it are what you invest in separately. Both matter. In that order.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Is this the same as 1:1 coaching?
No. The Inner Circle is my consistent presence in a group format — his voice, his perspective, his community. Coaching is a personalised relationship where I am embedded in your specific journey with accountability, plans, and direct access. Both matter. They serve different needs at different stages.
Will the membership replace buying my products?
No — and that's intentional. The Inner Circle gives you insight. The products give you structured tools for working through specific challenges. The membership makes you want the products. The products give you something the membership can't — a structured process you work through yourself at depth.
Who is the Inner Circle for?
Any music creative at any stage — musician, producer, engineer, songwriter, music entrepreneur, or someone who hasn't started yet. If you've been doing this alone and you want more than free content but aren't ready for 1:1 coaching, this is the right next step.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. No contracts, no questions. Cancel whenever you want and you keep access until the end of your billing period.
What happens if I become a coaching client?
Your Inner Circle membership is included with any coaching tier. You don't pay for both — the membership is absorbed into your coaching investment. You get everything in the Inner Circle plus everything in your coaching tier.
Is $19 a month worth it?
One reframe from the weekly audio that shifts how you see what's been stopping you is worth more than $19. One question answered on the monthly call that moves something stuck is worth more than $19. If you show up for it, it's worth it. That part is yours to decide.
